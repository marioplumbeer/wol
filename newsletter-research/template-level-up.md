# Template — Level Up (Ethan Evans): "Reader question → root cause → playbook"

Derived from ["Why There Are So Many Bad Managers"](https://levelupwithethanevans.substack.com/p/why-there-are-so-many-bad-managers).

```markdown
# ["Quoted first-person reader question" — or — "[Topic]: [Outcome promise]"]

*[3-part verb promise, e.g. "How to identify X, strengthen Y, and stop Z" —
the subtitle.]*

[Opening hook naming how common/relatable this problem is, then an immediate
reframe: "But it's not what you think — it's actually..."]

## [Section naming the surface-level problem]

[Establish the pattern readers recognize, with a supporting statistic cited
for credibility, e.g. "Roughly 70% of people who leave a job do so because
of..."]

## [Section naming the root cause]

[Explain *why* the problem exists structurally, not just anecdotally —
often an organizational/systemic explanation.]

## [Section: how companies/systems reinforce it]

[Show the incentive structure or catch-22 that keeps the problem alive.]

--- (course/cohort CTA banner, often placed near the top too) ---

## [Playbook section — paywalled]

[Concrete, numbered steps the reader can take, tailored to their level
(IC vs. manager vs. executive).]

---
*[Author bio with LinkedIn] · Subscribe · Sign in*
```

**Signature moves:** title voiced as the reader's own words or worry, subtitle always a 3-verb promise, argument builds problem → root cause → systemic reinforcement → prescriptive playbook, statistic dropped early for authority, course-sale CTA embedded in editorial content (monetization is visible, not just a footer ad).
