# Template — A Life Engineered: "Live coaching session"

Derived from ["I Coached a Security Engineer Through His Failed Meta Interview (Live)"](https://alifeengineered.substack.com/p/i-coached-a-security-engineer-through).

```markdown
# I Coached a [Role] Through His/Her [Failed Outcome] (Live)

*[The Easy Part Is X. Here's What Actually [Caused the Failure].]* — the subtitle.

[One-line intro naming the coachee (first name only) and the exact situation:
what they passed, what they failed, and the surface-level mystery — "he
walked out without knowing what went wrong."]

## What actually happened

[Narrative recap of the interview/situation as the coachee described it.]

## The real problem

[Reframe: the surface explanation the coachee believed vs. the real,
structural cause the coach identifies — e.g. "leading with methods instead
of outcomes."]

## The coaching, live

[Lightly-edited transcript or narrated walkthrough of the coaching
conversation — specific exchanges, the moment of realization.]

--- (paywall — video/full session gated) ---

## Key lessons for you

[Numbered or bulleted takeaways generalized from the specific session so
any reader can apply them, not just the coachee.]

---
*Subscribe to watch · Apply to be in the hot-seat for the next session ·
Previous Episodes carousel*
```

**Signature moves:** title names a real person's role + specific failure, subtitle reframes "the easy part" vs. "what actually loses it," structure moves from narrative → diagnosis → live transcript → generalized takeaways, closes with an application CTA inviting readers to become the next case study (unique to this format — turns readers into future content).
