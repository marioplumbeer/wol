# Template — Scarlet Ink: "Numbered Steps" advice post

Derived from ["8 Steps to Take After You've Given Notice"](https://www.scarletink.com/8-steps-to-take-after-youve-given-notice/).

```markdown
# [Cardinal number] [Steps/Things/Mistakes] to [achieve outcome / avoid pitfall]

*[One-sentence warning or promise — the subtitle.]*

> [Author bio line — ex-Amazon Tech Director and GM. X min read.]

[Cautionary opening line naming the naive assumption readers make, followed by
the real stakes: "It can mistakenly feel like X. But it's a small world, and..."]

## [Framing section: why this skill is underrated]

[Contrast with a *more* commonly-discussed skill the author is known for, to
set up why this lesser-discussed one matters — e.g. "I usually talk about
ramping up in new roles. Nobody talks about the exit."]

[Concrete negative-consequence examples: named companies, specific rejection
scenarios, a memorable one-liner — "Your reputation follows you, because this
industry, as big as it is, is very small."]

## 1. [First step, phrased as an imperative]

[Explanation + a short scenario illustrating what happens if you skip it.]

--- (paywall — remaining numbered steps are gated) ---

## 2. [Second step]
## 3. [Third step]
...
## N. [Final step]

[Closing line ties the list back to the opening warning — "leave like you
mean to work with these people again."]

---
*Subscribe · Related articles carousel*
```

**Signature moves:** opens by naming the underrated skill before listing it, first numbered item given in full for free (rest paywalled), concrete company/scenario examples for stakes, closing line echoes the opening warning.
