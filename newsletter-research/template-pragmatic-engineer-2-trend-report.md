# Template — The Pragmatic Engineer: "Trend report"

Derived from ["Headed for the Exit: the Great Engineering Leader Career Break"](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/the-great-engineering-leader-career-break).

```markdown
# [Evocative trend name]: the [Trend Description]

*[One-line stat/observation summarizing the trend — the subtitle.]*

[Personal anecdote opening: a specific conversation or data point that first
tipped the author off to the pattern. Ends with a reader-inclusive question:
"Just me, or is everyone seeing this?"]

[Methodology note: "Based on interviews with ~N [people in the trend]..."]

**In this article we cover the following reasons:**
1. [Reason 1]
2. [Reason 2]
3. [Reason 3]
...
N. [Reason N]
*(plus 1-2 additional discussion topics)*

---

## 1. [Reason 1, as a headline]

[Explanatory paragraph]

- [Supporting bullet/example]
- [Supporting bullet/example]

> "[Pull quote from an anonymous or named source]" — [Attribution, role]

## 2. [Reason 2]
## 3. [Reason 3]

--- (paywall — remaining reasons gated) ---

## [Reason N]

[Data visualization or chart referenced/embedded, e.g. job market demand.]

## [Closing discussion section]

[Broader implication for the industry / what this means going forward.]

---
*Subscribe · Already a paid subscriber? Sign in*
```

**Signature moves:** trend framed with a memorable name in the title, opens with the author's own "am I the only one noticing this" moment, states interview-count methodology upfront for credibility, numbered-reasons table of contents before the body, each reason mixes explanation + bullets + at least one pull quote from a real source, free preview stops partway through the numbered list.
