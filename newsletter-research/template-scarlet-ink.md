# Template — Scarlet Ink: "Metaphor → Concept → Application" essay

Derived from ["The Cheese Pizza Problem: Consensus Is Killing Your Best Decisions"](https://www.scarletink.com/why-big-groups-shouldnt-make-big-decisions-2/).

```markdown
# [Declarative title, often numbered or aphorism-style, ends in a period.]

*[One-sentence thesis restated in plain language — the subtitle/dek.]*

> [Author bio line, e.g. "David Anderson is an ex-Amazon Tech Director and GM. X min read."]

[Opening thesis statement — 1-2 sentences stating the claim baldly, no throat-clearing.]

## [Relatable, low-stakes metaphor to hook the reader]

[Everyday example (pizza for 25 people, painting a house) that illustrates the
core dynamic in miniature — no career jargon yet.]

[Bridge sentence: "The same thing happens at work when..."]

## [Corporate/career expansion of the metaphor]

[Name-drop recognizable examples (ChatGPT, Alexa, iPhone) to show the pattern
at scale. Introduce the key concept/mechanism (e.g. "ownership") that explains
*why* the metaphor holds.]

[Personal anecdote — a specific, named moment from the author's own career
that makes the concept concrete.]

--- (paywall line for premium content, if applicable) ---

## [Payoff / reframe section]

[Restate the lesson as a memorable rule of thumb or named concept the reader
can repeat to others.]

[Closing line: a short, quotable takeaway — often mirrors the subtitle.]

---
*Give it a ❤️ · Subscribe · [Related posts with lock icons]*
```

**Signature moves:** cold-open thesis statement (no preamble), single relatable metaphor stretched across the whole piece, named-concept coinage, first-person anecdote as proof, quotable one-liner close.
