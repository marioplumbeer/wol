# Template — A Life Engineered: "Borrowed quote → framework → exercise"

Derived from ["You're Not Bad at Hard Things"](https://alifeengineered.substack.com/p/youre-not-bad-at-hard-things).

```markdown
# [Reframe/counterintuitive title]

*[One-line restatement of the reframe — the subtitle.]*

> "[Opening quote from an external source — podcast, book, other thinker —
> that states the thesis better/more surprisingly than the author could.]"

[Author's admission or personal tension: "I used to conflate X and Y..."]

## [Concrete personal anecdote, specific and technical]

[A real, named work story (e.g. designing a batch processing system) that
grounds the abstract idea in lived experience.]

## [The framework, named and visualized]

[Introduce a simple mental model — often a 2x2 matrix or short taxonomy —
with an embedded diagram/image.]

[Apply the framework to a second domain, e.g. "This is the same lesson
Amazon scaling taught me about careers."]

## Try it yourself

[A numbered exercise instructing the reader to apply the framework to their
own situation right now.]

## [Closing reframe]

[Short "next time X happens, do Y" action loop — mirrors the subtitle,
gives a decision rule rather than a summary.]

---
*Give it a ❤️ · Subscribe · [Sponsor masterclass ad] · Comments*
```

**Signature moves:** opens with someone *else's* quote rather than the author's own claim, one grounded anecdote + one abstracted framework (often visualized), always ends with a reader-facing exercise, not just a lesson.
