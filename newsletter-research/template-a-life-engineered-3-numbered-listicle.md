# Template — A Life Engineered: "Numbered listicle"

Derived from ["5 Things World-Class Engineers Do That You Don't"](https://alifeengineered.substack.com/p/5-things-world-class-engineers-do).

```markdown
# [Cardinal number] Things [Elite Group] Do That You Don't

*[Restated promise: "Here is what the truly elite do differently."]* — the subtitle.

[Credibility-establishing opening: how the author gets unusual access to this
elite group — e.g. "One of the best things about doing my podcast is the
hours it buys me with engineers at the very top of the field..."]

[Framing line: these are patterns observed across many conversations/years,
not a single anecdote.]

## 1. [First behavior, phrased as a habitual action]

[Explanation of the behavior + why it's counterintuitive or easy to miss.]

[Short illustrative example or quote from an elite practitioner.]

--- (paywall — remaining items gated) ---

## 2. [Second behavior]
## 3. [Third behavior]
## 4. [Fourth behavior]
## 5. [Fifth behavior]

[Closing line: "Copy them." — a direct imperative telling the reader what
to do with the list.]

*Claim my free post / purchase a paid subscription · Subscribe*
```

**Signature moves:** title formula "[N] Things [Elite Group] Do That You Don't," opens with the author's unique-access credential (podcast interviews) rather than personal experience, first item given free as a taste, closing line is a blunt one-word-sentence imperative ("Copy them"), reused as a series (a "5 More Things..." sequel post exists too).
